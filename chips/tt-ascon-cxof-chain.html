Architectural Highlight — Byte-Wide FIFO Streaming

The chain wrapper is built around a byte-wide input/output FIFO that decouples the slow UART host interface from the wide internal ASCON-p[12] permutation datapath. Every boundary crossing between the serial host and the parallel engine is exactly 8 bits, eliminating the 256-bit-wide combinational mux that blocked timing closure in earlier iterations. This single architectural decision moved the worst-case setup slack at the slow-slow corner from −4.68 ns (open) to +1.75 ns (closed), while reducing total cell count and power versus the wider-datapath baseline.
